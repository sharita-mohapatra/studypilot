
# StudyPilot 🎓

An AI-powered exam preparation coach for engineering students.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Engineering students often struggle to organize their syllabus, understand lengthy notes, prepare for examinations, and identify the topics they need to revise. Switching between multiple study tools can make preparation time-consuming.

StudyPilot aims to bring these activities together in one platform using Artificial Intelligence.

## 🎯 Target Users

- Engineering students preparing for semester examinations.
- Students who want to understand their class notes more effectively.
- Students who need structured study plans and practice quizzes.
- Students who want to track their progress and weak topics.

## 🚀 MVP Features

### 1. Personalized Study Planner
Students enter their syllabus, exam date, and available study time. The app generates an editable study plan.

### 2. AI Notes Assistant
Students upload supported PDF notes and ask questions about their study material. Answers should be grounded in the uploaded content.

### 3. AI Practice Quiz
Students generate practice questions from their notes, submit answers, receive explanations, and track their scores.

## 🛠️ Planned Technology Stack

- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and Next.js
- Database and Authentication: Supabase
- AI Integration: A hosted Large Language Model API
- Version Control: Git and GitHub

## 🗺️ Development Roadmap

2. Build the frontend.
3. Integrate authentication and the database.
4. Develop AI-powered study features.
5. Test with engineering students.
